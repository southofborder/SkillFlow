## Description:

A Chinese-language personal productivity and time-management advisor that helps users plan tasks, manage time and energy, improve focus, and address procrastination.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bo-aibot](https://clawhub.ai/user/bo-aibot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals use this skill for Chinese-language coaching on task planning, time blocking, focus, procrastination, and energy-aware scheduling. It provides practical markdown guidance and follow-up questions rather than executing actions or managing data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad Chinese planning, task, time, or energy-management terms.

Mitigation: Use it for Chinese-language productivity coaching contexts and review routing behavior if a narrower activation scope is required.

Risk: Productivity coaching can be overly generic or mismatched to a user's constraints.

Mitigation: Treat the output as guidance to adapt, and ask follow-up questions about schedule, tasks, energy patterns, and commitments before relying on a plan.
