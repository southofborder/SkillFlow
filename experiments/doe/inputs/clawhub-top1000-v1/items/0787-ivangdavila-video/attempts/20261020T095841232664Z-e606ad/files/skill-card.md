## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill guides agents to process local media files and create derived outputs.

Mitigation: Use it only with media the user is authorized to edit or transcribe, and choose output paths and names deliberately.

Risk: Video and audio files may contain confidential or sensitive content.

Mitigation: Avoid processing confidential recordings unless local handling, storage, and retention are acceptable.

Risk: Transformations such as compression, cropping, captioning, or overwriting outputs can reduce quality or lose context.

Mitigation: Verify duration, file size, playability, captions, and aspect ratio before delivery, and preserve originals when practical.
