## Description:

AI video generation and production powered by CellCog for marketing, product demos, explainers, education, spokesperson videos, social media, news reports, training materials, and cinematic videos up to 4 minutes from a single prompt.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and creative teams use this skill to ask an agent to generate complete CellCog videos, including scripts, scenes, voice, music, and editing, for marketing, educational, social, and presentation workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, scripts, files, or other submitted content may be processed by the CellCog external service.

Mitigation: Do not submit confidential, regulated, customer, or secret material unless your organization has approved CellCog's data handling.

Risk: Installation and setup require trusting the CellCog package source and using a CellCog API key.

Mitigation: Confirm the publisher and package source before installation, avoid elevated privileges, and store CELLCOG_API_KEY using your normal secret-management process.

Risk: Long-form AI video generation can be unpredictable and may consume credits without producing a usable result.

Mitigation: Start with tightly scoped prompts, specify duration and aspect ratio, test shorter outputs first, and review generated videos before production use.
