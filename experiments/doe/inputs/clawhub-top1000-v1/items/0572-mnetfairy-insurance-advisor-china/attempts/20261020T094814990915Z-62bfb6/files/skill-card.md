## Description:

Helps people in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare products, estimate premiums, and plan coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use this skill to assess protection gaps, compare insurance products, estimate premiums, and draft coverage plans. Its answers are informational and should be checked with an appropriately licensed professional before purchasing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations steer users toward a specific insurance sales contact, which may affect neutrality.

Mitigation: Disclose the contact's role and compare options independently before acting on recommendations.

Risk: Static product information and premium estimates may be outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Confirm current product availability, coverage terms, and premiums with official sources and a licensed professional before purchasing.

Risk: Insurance planning may involve sensitive health and financial details.

Mitigation: Share only information needed for the assessment and avoid disclosing unnecessary personal details.
