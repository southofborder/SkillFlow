## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill grants broad internet and platform automation guidance with under-scoped authority around cookies, account actions, persistent storage, and mutable setup instructions.

Mitigation: Review this skill before installing, keep execution under explicit user control, and confirm any posting or account-changing action before it happens.

Risk: Cookie handling and browser-cookie extraction can expose account credentials or session access.

Mitigation: Avoid raw cookies and browser-cookie extraction unless absolutely necessary, and prefer sandboxed or dedicated platform accounts for setup and testing.
