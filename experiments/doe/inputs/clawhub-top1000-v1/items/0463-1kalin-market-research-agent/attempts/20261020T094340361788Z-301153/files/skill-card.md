Conduct structured market research on any industry, competitor, or opportunity. Analyze market size, trends, competitors, and customer segments with a proven framework.

## Use Case:

Founders, product managers, consultants, investors, and other external users use this skill to structure market research, competitor analysis, customer segmentation, strategic frameworks, and go-to-market recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill structures research but does not provide verified market data by itself, so market sizes, competitors, and trends may be incomplete or outdated without source checking.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims against reliable current sources and clearly separate confirmed facts from estimates and assumptions.

Risk: The README links to optional paid external context packs that are not automatically fetched or installed by the skill.

Mitigation: Review any external paid resource separately before using it as research evidence.
