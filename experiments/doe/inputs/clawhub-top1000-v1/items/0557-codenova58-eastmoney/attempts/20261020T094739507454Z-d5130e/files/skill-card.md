## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market data or news summaries may be incomplete, delayed, or unsuitable for financial decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational only and verify market data with authoritative sources before making financial decisions.

Risk: The skill could be misused beyond its intended public-information scope.

Mitigation: Use it only for public Eastmoney quote and news summaries; do not use it for authenticated areas, trading, account management, bulk collection, or automated financial actions.
