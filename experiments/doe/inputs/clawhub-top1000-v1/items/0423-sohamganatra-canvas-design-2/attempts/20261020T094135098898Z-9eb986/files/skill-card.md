## Description:

Creates original visual art as Markdown design philosophies and PDF or PNG canvas outputs guided by high-craft visual design principles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and designers use this skill to turn a creative prompt into a concise visual philosophy and a polished static artwork. It is suited for posters, art pieces, and other single-page visual designs where ideas are communicated primarily through composition, form, color, and restrained text.
