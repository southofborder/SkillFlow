## Description:

AI News ZH collects AI news from English technology sources, translates and organizes it into a Chinese daily briefing, and can send it to configured Feishu, Telegram, or Discord channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aizain](https://clawhub.ai/user/aizain)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External Chinese-speaking AI practitioners, researchers, and industry watchers use this skill to collect current AI news, remove duplicates, classify items, and receive an 8-12 item Chinese daily briefing through configured chat channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can send generated briefings to configured chat channels.

Mitigation: Verify the destination channel and run the skill manually once before enabling scheduled delivery.

Risk: Briefings depend on fetched public news sources and automated filtering, translation, and summarization.

Mitigation: Review initial output for source relevance, translation quality, duplicate handling, and links before relying on recurring reports.
