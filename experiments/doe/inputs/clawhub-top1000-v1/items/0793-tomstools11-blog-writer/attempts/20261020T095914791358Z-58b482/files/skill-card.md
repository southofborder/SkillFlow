## Description:

This skill helps agents draft blog posts, articles, and long-form content in a distinctive direct, conversational, opinionated writing style, from research review through Notion publication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tomstools11](https://clawhub.ai/user/tomstools11)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to turn topics, research notes, or links into blog posts and thought leadership articles that match the writer's established voice. The workflow also supports review, iteration, Notion publication, and saving finalized examples for future style calibration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft or unfinished writing may be sent to a fixed Notion database without fresh confirmation.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when Notion publication is intended, or edit it to require explicit confirmation and destination verification before every Notion write.

Risk: Private notes or sensitive research could be included in published draft content.

Mitigation: Avoid using the skill for private or sensitive material unless the workflow is changed to prevent automatic publication.
