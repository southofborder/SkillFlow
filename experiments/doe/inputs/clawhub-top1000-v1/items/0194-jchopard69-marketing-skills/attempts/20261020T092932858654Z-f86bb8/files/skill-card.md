## Use Case:

External marketers, founders, growth teams, and developers use this skill to route marketing requests to focused playbooks and produce campaign plans, CRO recommendations, SEO guidance, copy drafts, checklists, measurement plans, and implementation snippets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some modules describe use with connected ad accounts, social schedulers, customer-list uploads, analytics identifiers, or scraping tools without strong approval boundaries.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval and preview exact posts, accounts, schedules, budgets, audiences, data sources, and uploads before any external action.

Risk: Tracking, retargeting, lead capture, and scraping guidance can create privacy, consent, or platform-terms exposure.

Mitigation: Review applicable privacy law, consent requirements, platform terms, and permitted data sources before using those recommendations.

Risk: Marketing recommendations may depend on unavailable metrics, keyword volumes, claims, or competitor research.

Mitigation: Label assumptions, do not invent metrics or keyword volumes, and verify factual claims before publication or campaign launch.
