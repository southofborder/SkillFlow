## Description:

Locate anything with progressive search expansion, multi-source validation, and iterative refinement until found.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, employees, and external users can use this skill to guide an agent through finding a specific item when the source or location is unknown. It emphasizes clarifying success criteria, expanding search paths, validating results, and reporting confidence and caveats.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may make an agent more likely to continue iterative searches for vague find-style requests.

Mitigation: Ask for constraints such as source, budget, location, time, format, and success criteria before expanding the search.

Risk: Search results may be incorrect, outdated, or not the specific item the user requested.

Mitigation: Validate source reliability and currency before delivering results, and report confidence and caveats when uncertainty remains.
