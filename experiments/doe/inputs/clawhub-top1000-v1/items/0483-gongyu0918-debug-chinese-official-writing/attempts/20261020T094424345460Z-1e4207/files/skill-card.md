## Description:

Assists with drafting, revising, reviewing, and formatting Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Staff in public agencies, businesses, schools, and news organizations use this skill to draft and refine Chinese official documents, review existing copy, and prepare text for formal presentation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain incorrect facts, figures, policy references, dates, signatures, or formatting.

Mitigation: Review and verify each draft and its formal layout against authoritative materials before use.

Risk: Drafting and review may involve confidential or regulated source documents.

Mitigation: Handle supplied materials according to organizational data-handling rules.
