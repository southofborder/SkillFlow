## Description:

Helps draft, edit, and review external-facing writing so it reads naturally human, avoids AI tells, and skips corporate fluff.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[reighlan](https://clawhub.ai/user/reighlan)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Writers, editors, marketers, and agents use this skill to draft or revise publication-bound content such as articles, newsletters, marketing copy, website copy, and emails to lists. It is not intended for casual chat, code, internal notes, or quick replies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External writing polished with AI assistance may be subject to disclosure, authorship, or editorial policies.

Mitigation: Review the final content against the applicable publication, client, or organizational policy before release.

Risk: Style guidance can overcorrect a draft by removing useful nuance, attribution, or domain-specific wording.

Mitigation: Use the checklist as editorial guidance and keep a human review step for accuracy, tone, and context.
