## Description:

Diagnoses productivity breakdowns such as overwhelm, procrastination, scattered priorities, broken habits, overloaded meetings and messages, and role-specific constraints, then guides capacity math, prioritization, planning, delegation, reviews, and durable local memory updates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and teams use this skill to diagnose why personal productivity systems are failing and turn goals, projects, commitments, reviews, and habits into capacity-aware plans. It is especially aimed at users dealing with overwhelm, procrastination, focus loss, meetings and messages, delegation, burnout, ADHD, or role-specific constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may keep persistent local memory about work patterns, projects, contacts, commitments, and limited user-stated health facts.

Mitigation: Install only if that local memory is acceptable, periodically review the disclosed Clawic data folders, and avoid storing sensitive health, HR, financial, or credential material unless intentional.

Risk: The skill can automatically read and modify persistent productivity, project, contact, and health-related notes.

Mitigation: Review durable note changes during use and keep authoritative or sensitive records outside the disclosed productivity data paths when the skill should not manage them.
