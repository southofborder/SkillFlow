## Description:

国内外社会、科技、军事新闻汇总。自动搜索、筛选、整理新闻要点。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[httencn](https://clawhub.ai/user/httencn)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to gather, filter, and summarize domestic and international society, technology, and military news into a structured Chinese-first briefing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fast-moving or sensitive military news may contain incomplete, outdated, or unreliable claims.

Mitigation: Verify important claims against primary or highly reputable sources before relying on or sharing the summary.
