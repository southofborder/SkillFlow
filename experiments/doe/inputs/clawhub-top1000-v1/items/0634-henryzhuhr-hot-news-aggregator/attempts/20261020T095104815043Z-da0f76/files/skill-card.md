## Description:

Aggregates domestic and international society, technology, and military news from public sources, filters duplicate or lower-confidence items, and produces structured summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[henryzhuhr](https://clawhub.ai/user/henryzhuhr)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to collect recent public news across society, technology, and military topics and review concise, source-linked summaries organized by category.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad news-summary requests may activate the skill when a narrower information need was intended.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested topic scope and source categories before collecting or summarizing news.

Risk: Summaries of public news sources can omit context or reflect source errors.

Mitigation: Keep source links in the output and verify high-impact claims against official or authoritative reporting.
