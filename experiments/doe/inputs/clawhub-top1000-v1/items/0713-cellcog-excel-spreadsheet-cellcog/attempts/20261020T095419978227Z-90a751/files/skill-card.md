## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and business users use this skill to ask an agent to create Excel workbooks such as financial models, budgets, trackers, dashboards, charts, formulas, and formatted XLSX files through CellCog.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may submit confidential, regulated, or business-sensitive spreadsheet data to CellCog.

Mitigation: Confirm CellCog's data handling is acceptable before submitting sensitive workbook content or business assumptions.

Risk: The skill depends on the CellCog package and publisher identity being authentic.

Mitigation: Verify the skill and package come from the expected CellCog publisher, and prefer pinned or isolated installation when possible.

Risk: Generated formulas, projections, or financial models may contain incorrect assumptions or calculations.

Mitigation: Review formulas, model assumptions, and outputs before relying on generated spreadsheets for decisions.
