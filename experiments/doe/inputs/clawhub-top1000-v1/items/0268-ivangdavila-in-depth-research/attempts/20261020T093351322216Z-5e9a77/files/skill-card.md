## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and developers use this skill to plan and deliver deliberate multi-source investigations with documented search strategy, source evaluation, synthesis, caveats, gaps, and confidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can encourage broader web research and source logging than a quick lookup requires.

Mitigation: Invoke it for deliberate research tasks and set scope, depth, and time budget before searching.

Risk: Research outputs may still include incomplete or misleading conclusions if weak sources are over-weighted.

Mitigation: Use the documented source hierarchy, triangulate important claims, and preserve confidence, caveats, and gaps in the final report.
