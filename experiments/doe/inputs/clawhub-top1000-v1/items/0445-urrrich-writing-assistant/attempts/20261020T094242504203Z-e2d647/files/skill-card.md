## Description:

Writing Assistant coordinates specialized writing support by analyzing tasks, developing content strategy when references are available, and breaking down tasks into detailed guidance when references are absent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to guide writing tasks, including selecting references for content strategy or decomposing open-ended writing requests into detailed steps for a writing assistant workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill delegates writing work to writing tools configured in the agent environment, which may vary by installation.

Mitigation: Before installing, confirm that the available MCP writing tools are trusted and appropriate for the intended workflow.
