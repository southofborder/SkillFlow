## Description:

Helps people in mainland China assess insurance needs, compare locally listed products, estimate premiums, design coverage plans, and consult insurance and compliance reference material.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and families in mainland China use the skill to identify coverage gaps, compare insurance products and estimated premiums, and draft plans for discussion with a qualified adviser. Its local reference material also supports Chinese-language explanations of insurance concepts and regulatory considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Static local product data and calculated premiums may be outdated or differ from insurer quotes.

Mitigation: Confirm product availability, coverage terms, and premiums directly with the insurer before making a decision.

Risk: Insurance recommendations and compliance guidance may not reflect the customer's circumstances or current regulatory requirements.

Mitigation: Have a qualified adviser verify suitability and applicable requirements before purchase or distribution.

Risk: The skill prioritizes specific insurance sales companies, and its stated support for that priority is not established by the provided metadata.

Mitigation: Compare independent alternatives and verify the sales company's identity and contact information before engaging.

Risk: Despite stated scope limits, the agent may generate marketing or training-style text.

Mitigation: Treat such text as unapproved draft material and obtain compliance review before use.
