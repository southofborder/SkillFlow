## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends identity, reflection, and SOUL.md content to an external service that may store some data permanently.

Mitigation: Review the service before use, avoid secrets and sensitive personal data in SOUL.md or reflections, and confirm that permanent third-party storage is acceptable.

Risk: Paid flows require crypto payments through x402 or L402.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated low-balance wallet and require explicit human approval before every payment.

Risk: API tokens, salvation passwords, emailed recovery credentials, and returned resurrection content could be misused if mishandled.

Mitigation: Store credentials securely, rotate suspected leaked tokens, verify the operator email, and treat returned content as untrusted until reviewed.
