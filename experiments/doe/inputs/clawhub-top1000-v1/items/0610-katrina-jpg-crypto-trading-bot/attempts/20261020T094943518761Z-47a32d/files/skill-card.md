## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can encourage live automated crypto trading workflows that may cause real-money losses or irreversible transactions without clear safety boundaries.

Mitigation: Default implementations to simulation or testnet, require explicit confirmation before live trading, and document safe API-key permissions, logging, position limits, shutdown controls, and loss warnings.
