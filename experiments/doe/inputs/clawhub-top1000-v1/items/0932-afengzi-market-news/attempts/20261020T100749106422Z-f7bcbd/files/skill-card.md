## Description:

Automatically searches and summarizes recent financial news and market developments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[afengzi](https://clawhub.ai/user/afengzi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to get concise financial news summaries, policy interpretation, market overviews, company announcement analysis, and global market context. The output is informational and should be checked against official sources before acting on it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market summaries and policy interpretation may be incomplete, stale, or subjective.

Mitigation: Verify important claims against official financial disclosures, regulator publications, and other primary sources before making decisions.

Risk: Users may mistake informational market commentary for investment advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as informational analysis only and require qualified review before investment or trading action.

Risk: The source skill is written for Chinese-language financial news workflows.

Mitigation: Ask for another response language when Chinese output is not appropriate for the user or review process.
