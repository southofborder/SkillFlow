## Description:

AI tutoring and education powered by CellCog. Study guides, exam prep, coding tutorials, language learning, math help, science explanations, practice problems - every subject, every level. Explains concepts via diagrams, analogies, worked examples, and interactive lessons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners, educators, and developers use this skill to ask CellCog for concept explanations, worked examples, study guides, practice questions, coding tutorials, and language-learning support across subjects and levels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts sent through this skill may go to CellCog and could include private study, workplace, or personal material.

Mitigation: Do not include private or regulated information unless sending it to CellCog is acceptable for the user's context.

Risk: Setup commands and package installation can introduce supply-chain or privilege risk.

Mitigation: Install CellCog only from trusted sources, prefer pinned or reviewed versions, and avoid unnecessary administrative privileges.

Risk: Tutoring responses may be incomplete or misleading for high-stakes academic, legal, medical, financial, or professional decisions.

Mitigation: Use the output as educational guidance and verify important answers against authoritative sources or qualified instructors.
