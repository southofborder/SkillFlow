## Description:

Operates as the designer on a job: decides what to make, makes it, defends it in review, and hands over files that can actually be built.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and design practitioners use this skill to produce and review practical design work for brands, product interfaces, components, marketing surfaces, mobile apps, print pieces, accessibility checks, research findings, and engineering handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill automatically stores and reuses local design, client, project, and licence information.

Mitigation: Review the configured Clawic data folders before installation and use the skill only when maintaining a local design memory is intended.

Risk: Shared contacts, projects, and finance notes may include sensitive client or business context.

Mitigation: Require user confirmation before updates to shared contacts, projects, or subscriptions, and avoid storing contracts, bank or tax details, credentials, participant identities, or sensitive client context.
