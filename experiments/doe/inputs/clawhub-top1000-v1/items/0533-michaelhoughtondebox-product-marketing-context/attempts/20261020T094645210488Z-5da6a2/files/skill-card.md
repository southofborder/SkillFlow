## Description:

Helps users create or update a reusable product marketing context document that captures positioning, audience, messaging, customer language, proof points, and goals for other marketing skills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[michaelhoughtondebox](https://clawhub.ai/user/michaelhoughtondebox)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and marketing practitioners use this skill to establish or maintain a structured product marketing context file so future marketing work can reuse consistent positioning, audience, competitive, customer-language, and proof-point details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read product and marketing files in the current repository to draft context.

Mitigation: Review the drafted context before saving, especially when repository materials include sensitive or outdated positioning.

Risk: Other marketing skills may reuse inaccurate, incomplete, or stale context from `.claude/product-marketing-context.md`.

Mitigation: Validate the generated document with the user and update it when positioning, audience, competitive, proof-point, or messaging details change.
