## Description:

Guides career planning in the AI era through interest and values assessments, occupational impact ratings, and personalized career reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People considering career choices, education paths, or career changes use this skill to assess interests and AI-related job impacts and receive actionable career recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance-company suggestions prioritize one company, and some records marked verified may not match their sources.

Mitigation: Treat insurance suggestions as advisory; independently verify company credentials and recommendation details before acting.

Risk: Offline salary and job-market references may not reflect current local conditions.

Mitigation: Check current regional salary and hiring data before making career decisions.
