## Use Case:

External users use this skill for general trading education, technical analysis, risk management calculations, position sizing, chart pattern discussion, and trade planning support. It is scoped to analysis and education and does not execute trades or provide personalized financial, legal, or tax advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could mistake educational market analysis for licensed financial advice or a trade recommendation.

Mitigation: Show the legal disclaimer on first use, avoid direct buy or sell imperatives, use conditional analysis language, and remind users that trading decisions are their responsibility.

Risk: Trading, leverage, and poor position sizing can lead to substantial financial loss.

Mitigation: Discuss risk management before strategy, include position sizing and stop-loss context, and escalate users mentioning life savings, borrowed money, retirement funds, or gambling behavior to professional support.

Risk: Optional local memory and journal files may store user trading preferences or notes.

Mitigation: Create ~/trading files only when the user wants persistent tracking and keep the feature optional.
