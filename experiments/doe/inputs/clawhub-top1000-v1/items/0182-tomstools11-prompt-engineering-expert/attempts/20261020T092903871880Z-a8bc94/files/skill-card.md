## Use Case:

Developers, prompt authors, and agent builders use this skill to review, generate, refine, and troubleshoot prompts, system prompts, and custom agent instructions. It helps produce clearer prompt guidance, reusable templates, evaluation criteria, and implementation advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Refusal-troubleshooting guidance could be misused to reframe harmful requests as legitimate prompts.

Mitigation: Use the guidance only to clarify benign intent and keep platform safety limits intact; do not use it to obtain harmful instructions.

Risk: Prompt recommendations may be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable for critical workflows without validation.

Mitigation: Test refined prompts with representative cases and apply human review before deployment in important applications.
