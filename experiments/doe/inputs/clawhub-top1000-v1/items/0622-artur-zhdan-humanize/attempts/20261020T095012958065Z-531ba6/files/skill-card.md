## Description:

Removes AI writing patterns from text so editing, reviewing, and rewriting sound more natural and human-written.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[artur-zhdan](https://clawhub.ai/user/artur-zhdan)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and agents use this skill to rewrite drafts that sound AI-generated while preserving meaning and intended tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewritten text may change tone or nuance when the skill adds voice or opinions.

Mitigation: Review the revised text against the source and keep edits that preserve the intended meaning and tone.
