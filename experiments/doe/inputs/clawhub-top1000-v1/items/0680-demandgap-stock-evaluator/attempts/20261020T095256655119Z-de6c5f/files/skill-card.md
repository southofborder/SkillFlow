## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External investors, analysts, and agent users use this skill to evaluate prospective stock purchases, compare watchlist candidates, and receive structured buy, hold, or sell recommendations with entry prices, sizing, risks, alternatives, and dashboard outputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce specific buy, sell, and position-sizing guidance that users may rely on for real-money decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational analysis, independently verify every metric and assumption, and evaluate decisions against personal risk tolerance or qualified financial advice.

Risk: Realistic sample dashboard values in the artifact could be mistaken for current market data.

Mitigation: Replace sample values with sourced current data before use, cite the data source, and use N/A when a metric cannot be verified.
