## Use Case:

Solopreneurs and business operators use this skill to plan practical content marketing work: choosing goals, researching audience needs, defining content pillars, selecting formats and channels, scheduling production, distributing content, repurposing assets, and measuring results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to appear for general content-planning prompts.

Mitigation: Use it when the user is asking for content strategy, content calendars, distribution, repurposing, or performance measurement guidance.

Risk: Content marketing recommendations can be incomplete or poorly matched to a specific business if the agent lacks audience, channel, or goal context.

Mitigation: Ask for business model, target audience, current channels, primary goal, and available production capacity before turning the framework into a plan.
