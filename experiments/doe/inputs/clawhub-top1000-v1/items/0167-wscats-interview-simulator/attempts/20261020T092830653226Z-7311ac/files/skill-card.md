## Description:

Simulates mock interviews for any role and experience level with tailored technical, behavioral, and case questions plus detailed feedback and scoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wscats](https://clawhub.ai/user/wscats)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Candidates and interview coaches use this skill to run structured mock interviews for a chosen role, seniority level, focus area, and session length. It asks one question at a time, adapts difficulty, critiques answers, and produces per-question feedback plus a final scorecard.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume-based interview setup can expose unnecessary personal information, confidential employer details, or identifiers in the chat.

Mitigation: Remove nonessential personal information before sharing a resume and keep resume excerpts limited to the experience and skills needed for the mock interview.

Risk: Because activation is conversational, the skill may begin an interview flow when the user only mentions interview-like terms casually.

Mitigation: Use clear activation phrasing such as 'mock interview for...' when starting a session and clarify intent before sharing sensitive preparation material.
