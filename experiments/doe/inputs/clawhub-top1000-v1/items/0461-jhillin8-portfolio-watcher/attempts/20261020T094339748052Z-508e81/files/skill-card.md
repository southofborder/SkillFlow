## Use Case:

External users use this skill to track stock, ETF, and cryptocurrency holdings through conversational requests, including price checks, target alerts, and portfolio gain/loss summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide sensitive holdings, purchase prices, or cost-basis details without clear privacy and storage controls.

Mitigation: Collect only the minimum portfolio details needed, explain storage and alert behavior before retaining data, and provide clear ways to review or delete saved holdings and alerts.

Risk: Portfolio performance summaries may be mistaken for financial advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses informational, avoid personalized investment recommendations, and preserve the skill's stated not-financial-advice posture.
