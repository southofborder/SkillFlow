## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Vercel deployment, removal, domain, purchase, transfer, promotion, rollback, and environment-variable commands can affect production projects or expose sensitive configuration.

Mitigation: Confirm the target project, team, and environment before executing commands, review production-impacting actions explicitly, and keep secret files such as .env.local out of logs and version control.
