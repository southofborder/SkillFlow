## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill routes Instagram scraping through Apify as a third-party processor.

Mitigation: Use it only when Apify processing is acceptable for the data and account context involved.

Risk: The workflows can collect personal, social, location-linked, and contact data.

Mitigation: Limit collection to lawful, authorized purposes and avoid follower, email, comment, or location-linked scraping unless those fields are necessary.

Risk: The setup instructions include tracked paid-plan links and a prescribed paid-plan failure message.

Mitigation: Review referral links and paid-plan messaging before deployment so users receive neutral purchasing guidance.
