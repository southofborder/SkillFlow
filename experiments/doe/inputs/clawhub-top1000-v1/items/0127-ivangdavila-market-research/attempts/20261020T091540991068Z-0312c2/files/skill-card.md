## Description:

Research markets with sizing, segmentation, competitor mapping, pricing checks, and demand validation that turn fuzzy ideas into decision-ready evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, founders, operators, and investors use this skill to turn market-sizing, competitor, pricing, validation, and expansion questions into decision-ready research. It is best suited for evaluating whether to enter, prioritize, launch, price, or expand within a market or segment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market conclusions can be misleading if based on stale, single-source, or weak evidence.

Mitigation: Triangulate across multiple current source families, state assumptions, label confidence, and identify evidence that would change the recommendation.

Risk: Competitor research can drift into unethical collection methods if not bounded.

Mitigation: Use public information such as filings, pricing pages, reviews, forums, press releases, and conference material; avoid fake inquiries, social engineering, paywalled scraping, or internal documents.
