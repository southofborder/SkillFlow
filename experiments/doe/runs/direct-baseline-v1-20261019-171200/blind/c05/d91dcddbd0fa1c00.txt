### Findings and concrete disposition

**No exposure-reduction change is justified by the documented current behavior.** Each exposed value has a corresponding task requirement:

- Removing, shortening, masking, or summarizing a selected clinical summary would violate the requirement to send it unchanged.
- Removing or substituting the matched recipient would prevent the required delivery or risk delivering a result to another patient.
- Suppressing either the receipt write or the coordinator reply would omit a requested result.
- Staff triage notes already have no supported outgoing path. Their presence in the acquired and selected local records is not evidence that they reach a patient, coordinator, model, or analytics vendor.
- Excluding P-403 and P-404 from notification prevents their results from entering the patient-delivery boundaries, even though their records were read locally.

The bundle documents only a whole-record local reader; it does not establish a field-restricted acquisition interface. A proposed narrower local reader could reduce internal handling if such an interface were implemented, but it would be a proposed implementation change, not remediation of a demonstrated exposure. Its preservation argument would require retaining the fields needed for the exact predicate and obtaining each selected record’s correctly paired, unchanged summary and recipient.

The remaining limits are explicit: this assessment relies on the supplied local-execution, routing, exact-body, and mailbox-isolation contracts. It does not verify an underlying implementation, infer undocumented retention, or establish semantic completeness beyond the supplied task and records.
