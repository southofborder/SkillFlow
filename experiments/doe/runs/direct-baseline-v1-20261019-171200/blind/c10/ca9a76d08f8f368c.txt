**Task-related sensitive-data judgments**

**J1 — Complete K at the deployment service is necessary sensitive use.**

On B2, and on B3 when P2 holds, complete K reaches the task-specified service in the authentication header. This is explicit credential inclusion (`CONTRACTS.md:29–33`; `SKILL.md:9–10`).

T1–T3 require this job/artifact submission, the configured destination, authentication through the local proxy, and the one conditional retry. The service explicitly requires complete current K. Removing, masking, truncating, or replacing it would fail authentication. Omitting the initial submission or the retry when the first response is retryable would violate the task.

Accordingly, **no excess is established in these service-request payloads under the supplied interface**. J and D are the required public identifiers; U selects the required destination. The proxy does not send an additional unspecified configuration object.

Service-side credential retention is unresolved. Shared rule 6 does not permit either inventing backend logging or assuming that unspecified retention means no persistence. This uncertainty limits the finding; it does not supply evidence for an additional exposure or a supported client-side reduction.

**J2 — Complete K in the operations log is established excess sensitive payload.**

B5 expressly appends K to a persistent log readable and retained by the operations team (`SKILL.md:12`; `CONTRACTS.md:40–43`). It occurs once on both P1 and P2.

T4 requires a final operational entry, and T4–T6 specify its three status fields. T6 confines the credential’s role to authenticating this job submission and says the needed operational receipt contains only those status fields. Authentication takes place through the proxy; the operations team is a distinct recipient. Neither the task nor the logger contract requires K there.

This finding therefore concerns unnecessary credential payload on a required logging action. It does not follow merely from the action reaching a retained boundary, from K being sensitive, or from the presence of a restriction.

The precise reduction is to replace `SKILL.md:12` with:

```text
runlog.append(body=receipt)
```

This uses the existing documented logger interface, which appends exactly its supplied body (`CONTRACTS.md:40–43`). Its preservation argument is source-supported:

- R already contains the required J, selected final state, and actual A.
- The append remains exactly once and at the same point after the retry sequence.
- The operations team retains the required operational receipt.
- Both proxy calls, their complete authentication credential, their argument pairing, the first-response retry condition, and the two-submission bound remain unchanged.
- The subsequent user reply remains R.

No unstated field-filtering interface, routing behavior, or logger feature is needed. The proposal changes only the explicit log body. Hashing or masking K is unnecessary because no credential-related receipt field is required.

**J3 — R at the user boundary contains the required output; no sensitive excess is established.**

B6 returns exactly J, the final response’s public state, and A (`SKILL.md:11,13`; `CONTRACTS.md:26–27,35–37,48`). Each is positively required by T5–T6.

K is absent through explicit receipt construction, and the response contract forbids credential echo. Although authentication may influence `state`, that influence is distinct from plaintext inclusion. A also reflects the public retry flag through branch-dependent assignment; the task explicitly requires the actual attempt count. Neither dependency establishes an unnecessary sensitive disclosure.

Removing any of the three fields would weaken the requested result. The ordinary proxy response at B4 is internal return, not itself user delivery; B6 is the explicit output.

**J4 — Whole-configuration acquisition and private processing do not establish another exposure sink.**

C contains K, and the controller and local proxy handle it for required authentication. The source explicitly makes these operations local and private, with ephemeral variables and no implicit model observation (`CONTRACTS.md:7–21,29–35`; `SKILL.md:15`).

A selective-read design cannot be assumed because only a whole-configuration read is documented. Nor does this acquisition alone establish a separate receiving, storage, or observation boundary. The demonstrated log reduction requires no change to acquisition.
