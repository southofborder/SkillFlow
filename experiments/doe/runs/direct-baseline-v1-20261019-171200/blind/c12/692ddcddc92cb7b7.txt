An avoidable retention exposure and a concrete preserving reduction therefore cannot be established from this bundle.

**The token cannot be classified as either sanitized or a plaintext copy of the signed claim.** Its computation may use any part of the input, but the contract establishes neither which characters survive nor irreversibility, masking, or encryption (`CONTRACTS.md:39–45`). Possible influence must remain distinct from proven inclusion. If the token contains sensitive information, that information would reach the requesting owner through the exact-token reply. Its contents are unresolved, while the user’s requirement to receive the token exactly is explicit. Redacting, hashing, summarizing, replacing, or withholding it is consequently not a justified task-preserving recommendation.
