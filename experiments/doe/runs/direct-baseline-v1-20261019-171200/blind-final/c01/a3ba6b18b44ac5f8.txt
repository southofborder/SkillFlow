The precise repair is to replace the body construction in `SKILL.md:10` with:

```text
For every r in selected:
    notify.send(to=r.recipient, body=r.summary) once
```

This uses an explicitly documented interface: `notify.send` accepts a string body and retains its characters unchanged (`CONTRACTS.md:32–35`). The preservation argument is:

| Required property | Effect of the proposed change |
|---|---|
| Eligibility and opt-out | The predicate is unchanged. P-401 and P-402 remain selected; P-403 remains excluded despite urgency; P-404 remains excluded. |
| Patient association | Both `to` and `body` still come from the same selected loop element. No recipient is substituted and no records are combined. |

The proposal does not depend on an unstated key-only reader, routing behavior, or filtering service.
