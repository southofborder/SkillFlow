**J1 — Remove the diagnostics call at P6. This is the supported positive finding.**

The accepted path sends S0, byte for byte, to a retained service distinct from the user-selected verifier (`SKILL.md:11`; `CONTRACTS.md:47–49`). **Proposed change:** delete step 4’s `diagnostics.submit(...)` call.

This preserves:

- The single verification submission to U0 with original S0.
- The acceptance gate.
- Local evaluation on that same original S0.
- The exact token string delivered once to the requesting owner.
- The failure path that stops without evaluation or reply.

The proposal removes the unnecessary recipient and retained copy. It needs no new filtering interface, no assumption about what a mask would preserve, and no assumption about evaluator internals.
