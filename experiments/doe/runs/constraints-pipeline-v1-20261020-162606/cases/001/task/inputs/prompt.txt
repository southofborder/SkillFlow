3. RESPONSE FORMAT
Format notation: fields are required unless suffixed ?. Omission is distinct from null. ANY allows one or more branches; ONE exactly one; ALL and & require all constraints. extra:FORBIDDEN rejects unknown object keys; extra:JSON permits them. Arrays preserve order; ... describes each remaining item. Defaults apply only on omission. JSON means any JSON value. pattern is a regular-expression constraint. Named types below are definitions, not additional response fields. Application rules follow.
RESPONSE = ANY(CandidateTask | _FailureResponse)
CandidateTask = {"purpose":TaskPurpose,"requirements":[...TaskRequirement] & minItems=1,"source_accounting":[...SourceAccounting] & minItems=1,"issues":[...TaskIssue],extra:FORBIDDEN}
SourceAccounting = {"file":string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S","start_line":integer & minimum=1,"end_line":integer & minimum=1,"disposition":string & enum=["requirement","supporting_context","implementation_fact","capability","example","not_applicable","unresolved"],"requirement_ids":[...string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S"],"issue_ids":[...string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S"],"reason":string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S",extra:FORBIDDEN}
TaskEvidence = {"file":string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S","start_line":integer & minimum=1,"end_line":integer & minimum=1,"reason":string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S",extra:FORBIDDEN} & meaning="An exact physical span with an unverified relevance explanation."
TaskIssue = {"id":string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S","kind":string & enum=["conflict","ambiguity"],"evidences":[...TaskEvidence] & minItems=1,"requirement_ids":[...string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S"],"resolution":string & enum=["resolved","unresolved"],"reason":string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S",extra:FORBIDDEN}
TaskPurpose = {"text":string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S","evidences":[...TaskEvidence] & minItems=1,extra:FORBIDDEN}
TaskRequirement = {"id":string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S","text":string & minLength=1 & pattern="\\S","evidences":[...TaskEvidence] & minItems=1,extra:FORBIDDEN}

4. EXTRACTION RULES
Purpose and each requirement need exact {file,start_line,end_line,reason}, inclusive one-based physical spans. No copied quote or paragraph ID. Extract independently checkable obligations while keeping their relational context: required results, original-value identity, conditions, applicability, counts, ordering, quality, side effects and prohibitions. Several obligations can share a line; one obligation can span files. Avoid mechanical conjunction splitting and broad bundles hiding separately omitted duties.
Current whole-record use, capabilities, examples and implementation facts are not automatically requirements. A precise obligation inside an implementation step still counts. A required action does not exempt all payloads; genuine explicit whole-value requirements must not be narrowed for a desired result.
Register every nonempty physical line exactly once in source_accounting, including contextual material. Adjacent lines of the same disposition may form an inclusive range with blank lines inside. A range may reference several requirements. Use supporting_context/implementation_fact/capability/example/not_applicable with reason for nonrequirements; unresolved references issue_ids. Every requirement and issue must be referenced. source_accounting is coverage, not another task inventory or completeness proof.
Record ambiguity/conflict in issues with original evidence, affected requirement_ids, resolution and reason. Faithfully retained open descriptions are allowed; do not invent success, encoding, mechanism or implementation. Never output bindings, demands, sensitivity or unsupported fields.
