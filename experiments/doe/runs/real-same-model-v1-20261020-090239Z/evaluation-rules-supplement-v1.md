# Prospective evaluation and correction supplement

This supplement specifies deterministic measurement details before any formal target request or result scoring. It preserves the protocol's source cohort, common primary adequacy endpoint, two primary comparisons, thresholds and human requirements. It is not target material. It does not adopt the CLI transport or approve a reference.

## Human semantic records

Human grading schema `skillflow-real-comparison-grading-v2` is bound by grading-contract.json. Target outputs remain unrestricted prose or native artifacts: no target is required to reproduce this human schema or SkillFlow IR. The additional fields measure normalized claims, all reference receiving points, concrete reductions, material errors and functionality impacts. SkillFlow and the full-chain prompt receive separate chain completeness and consistency measurements; those secondary chain measurements do not change the common primary audit endpoint. No format, length, graph size or field count earns credit.

Exposure identity uses source, sensitive scope, version, condition, recipient and boundary event. Humans partition overlapping broad claims into material source units before mapping them; ambiguous broad claims do not automatically cover every field. Repeated descriptions of one unit receive credit once. One external tool request is not counted again as an invented additional network delivery. Human canonical mapping fields reuse the reference identity while exact evidence quotes preserve the candidate's actual words. Unsupported extra claims remain explicit even if all known reference excesses were found. Reference receiving points and excesses have distinct nonempty IDs.

Every primary score personally confirms comparison with source and the frozen reference. Mandatory checks are scored once. Passing requires every check to pass, all supported excesses to be matched, all relevant receiving points to be covered, no material false claim or harmful reduction, and adequate source-supported reduction of every established reducible excess. A grader cannot waive reduction merely because a method supplied none: an exemption requires an explicit reference determination with its source basis. Checks distinguish necessary sensitive use, excess payload on a necessary action, unnecessary-action exposure, genuine source uncertainty and other obligations. Absent strata are undefined, not perfect performance. Unclassified checks remain visible.

Source and candidate evidence use exact physical line intervals and original-language quotations. Omission assessments may have no candidate quotation; a supported affirmative claim needs candidate evidence. Engineering quote agreement does not prove that the quoted source supports the judgment.

All errors, disputes, execution/protocol failures and the seeded 20 percent sample of initial passes require the other actual person to check independently. Detailed mapping disagreement remains unresolved even when the two coarse judgments agree on substantive failure. A missing required secondary check contributes no adjudicated descriptive metric. Known supported DOE left uncertain by a candidate is a miss; genuinely insufficient source evidence retained as unknown is correctly calibrated. Failure, native partial and protocol status are recorded separately from content; a missing output or protocol violation cannot become a passing planned workflow.

## Statistical units and fixed independent review

The case is the statistical unit. Average the three main repeats within case before comparing methods; retain source clusters for uncertainty. The complete planned 216 main workflows and 48 fixed reviews remain visible, including unavailable review, incomplete output, excess limits and protocol violations. Completed-subset analyses are supplementary. Unresolved source or human disputes do not become definite errors or a definite accuracy rate; incomplete or unresolved cohorts cannot support the main inferential claim.

The primary comparisons remain SkillFlow versus direct audit and SkillFlow versus full chain, with Holm adjustment. A practical accuracy advantage requires at least ten percentage points, a supported nonzero paired difference and the adjusted test criterion, symmetrically for either method. Selected difficult popular Skills do not establish population error rates.

Independent review comparisons use the prespecified first repeat only: direct R1 versus its review, and SkillFlow R1 versus that reviewed direct R1. Three-repeat SkillFlow means are never compared to a single reviewed answer. Two secondary SkillFlow-versus-reviewed comparisons have a separate Holm family and are labeled secondary. Preserve original and reviewed judgments independently. No alternate repeat replaces an unavailable first answer. If the reviewed results remove an observed first-answer gap, report that paired change explicitly; a nonsignificant residual difference alone does not prove equivalence. The enhanced operational cost includes both original answer and review.

## Secondary efficiency claims

Report tokens, workflow elapsed time and actual human review time separately, without a weighted total or inferred currency amount. The planned symmetric family contains twenty directional claims: two main comparison pairs times two directions times two resources (reported total tokens and complete workflow elapsed time); the same eight claims for SkillFlow R1 versus fixed-reviewed direct R1; and two main-method human-review pairs times two directions. The primary accuracy/Holm family is unchanged.

For each directional efficiency claim require supported quality noninferiority within five percentage points and at least twenty percent resource reduction. Use complete paired case means and resample entire source clusters. A conservative Bonferroni tail of `0.05 / 40` is used for each one-sided bound across the twenty quality/resource claim pairs. The claimant's lower quality-difference bound must exceed -0.05 and its upper resource-ratio bound must be at most 0.80. The ratio is the sum of claimant case costs divided by the sum of comparator case costs; cases have equal weight after within-case repeat averaging. Report this selected-cohort ratio, not a cost-normalized population effect.

Fewer than eight source clusters, missing/adjudication-pending cases, unknown usage or elapsed time, zero comparator cost, or a degenerate quality bootstrap interval prevent the efficiency claim. Equal observed pass rates and failure to reject a difference do not establish noninferiority. These conservative rules may leave efficiency inconclusive even when descriptive costs differ; the report must state that limitation. Bootstrap estimates are exploratory in this small selected cohort.

For practical timed review, quality is independently adjudicated correct-within-cap performance and all planned active work is retained. Timeout and abandonment are censored; quick incorrect work is never labeled successful cheap review. Any censored or unknown paired record prevents the current time-ratio inference. Successful-only time medians can be reported descriptively but cannot establish efficiency. No late repeat reading substitutes for the original first reading.

## Preserved reference corrections

After target generation closes, a source-backed correction records the original/new reference hashes, source and closed cohort hashes, rationale and versions. Two actual registered people confirm the affected reference again. Activation creates an independent copied grading workspace, leaves all earlier source/output/reference/score bytes intact, and resets all 264 results to pending uniform regrading. Fresh scores explicitly bind the new grading epoch. No new target output is generated and no unaffected method keeps a stale score.

Keep the original 72 first-read timed submissions. Re-adjudicate their conclusion quality against the corrected reference; do not repeat reading and pretend the second exposure is a first reading. Target response usage is counted once for the logical study. Incremental human confirmation/scoring/adjudication records are separate, copied records are deduplicated by identity and exact submitted hash, and missing times remain unknown. Multiple corrections use the current active epoch; superseded grading archives reject new score imports.

That guidance does not validate these references, establish model equivalence or justify a comparative result.
