5: All-in-one TikTok Shop data intelligence assistant. Search products, discover trending items, analyze influencers, explore shops, track video performance, and get ad insights — all through natural language.
6: 
7: ## Features
8: 
9: - **Product Search** — Search TikTok Shop products by keyword, category, price, sales volume, with multi-market support
10: - **Sales Rankings** — Sales ranking, new products, managed (sShop) ranking, hot promotion ranking
11: - **Product Detail** — Deep dive into any product's sales trends, influencer partnerships, video performance, reviews
12: - **Influencer Analysis** — Search and analyze TikTok creators: followers, engagement, sales performance, fan demographics
13: - **Video Analytics** — Hot video search, video-product ranking, video detail with trend data
14: - **Shop Intelligence** — Search shops, view product lineup, analyze influencer partnerships
15: - **Ad & Creative Insights** — E-commerce ad search, advertiser analysis, trend insights, top keywords
16: - **Deep Research** — AI-powered deep analysis for complex queries. Automatically triggered for multi-dimensional analysis, returns structured HTML reports
